      Locker terms

      • A locker is rented for one evening's time: 6-8 PM, 9-11 PM or both on a JCC night, 7-9 PM on a Petromin night. The key stays with MicroMobility staff, who open it for you.
      • Do not put food or open drinks, anything flammable, dangerous or illegal, or any weapon in a locker.
      • Nothing may be left overnight: collect everything the same evening.
      • Things left behind are kept for 14 days, then donated or disposed of.
      • MicroMobility is not responsible for loss of or damage to anything kept in a locker.
      • A locker reserved and not collected is held until staff free it; a wallet payment for it is then refunded.
